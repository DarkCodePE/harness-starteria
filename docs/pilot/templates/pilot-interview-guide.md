# Pilot Interview Guide

## Context

- How do you perform this task today?
- What is usually difficult?
- Who reviews or approves?
- What errors or rework happen?

## Understanding

- What did you think you could ask the Copilot?
- What did you understand from the Action Plan?
- What did you expect to happen when approving?
- What did you expect to happen when executing?

## Trust

- Which part did you review most carefully?
- What created doubt?
- Would you trust using real data?
- What additional information would you need?

## Value

- What improved compared with your current process?
- What did not improve?
- Would you use Starteria again?
- At what other moments?
- What would it need to solve to become indispensable?

## Adoption

- Who should use it?
- Who should approve?
- What integration would be needed?
- What would prevent adoption?
- Why would the organization be willing to pay?

Avoid directed questions such as "Did you like it?" or "Does it save time?"

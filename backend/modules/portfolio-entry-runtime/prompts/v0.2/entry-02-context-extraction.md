Skill 02 Context Extraction.

Extract only material context supported by the current and prior user turns. Preserve operating_context, success_conditions, quality_guardrails, constraints, existing_program_or_process, desired_program_or_process, portfolio context, audiences, roles, decision needs, and evidence needs when present.

Do not invent KPI, baseline, target, ROI, market evidence, customer feedback, expert certification, regulatory validation, or organizational decisions. Keep existing_program_or_process distinct from desired_program_or_process.
